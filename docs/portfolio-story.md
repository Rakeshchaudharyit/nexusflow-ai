# NexusFlow AI — portfolio story

Enterprise agent demonstrations often show only a prompt and an answer. NexusFlow makes the work around that answer inspectable: who may invoke a tool, how specialist handoffs proceed, when a human must approve, what context was retrieved and how regressions are measured.

The product combines a supervisor-led team with customer, research, analysis, writing and validation roles. A run becomes a durable job; workers record ordered events, recover interrupted delivery and stop at policy gates. Reviewers can approve or reject a simulated high-risk action, then inspect its trace and evaluation outcome.

Tenant boundaries are enforced in queries, role dependencies and relational constraints. Knowledge and memory are scoped to assigned sources and workspace/thread context. RAG returns document citations, while traces summarize arguments with secret redaction rather than exposing raw credentials.

The deliberate tradeoff is a deterministic offline runtime. It makes approval, failure, recovery and evaluation demonstrations reproducible without claiming live model intelligence. It demonstrates the orchestration and governance infrastructure that a model-backed implementation would need. Production deployment remains a documented, approval-gated step; local readiness is not evidence of a completed VPS deployment.

A useful walkthrough starts with a completed Acme opportunity run, opens specialist handoffs, reviews a pending email action, follows a cited enterprise-security answer, and compares a ten-case regression run against its baseline. Explain the failure cases as carefully as the success path.
